风险冲击下供应链配置策略与韧性提升知网

祝树金、曾聃　《经济研究》，2025，60(8)：94–115

优化供应链配置策略是提升产业链供应链韧性的重要途径。本文以2020年全球性公共卫生事件为外生冲击，基于中国上市公司的供应商—客户数据，研究系统性风险冲击下供应链配置策略对企业供应链韧性的影响。研究表明：集中化配置能够有效缓解系统性风险对企业供应链表现的负面影响；机制检验发现，集中化配置通过提高供应链灵活性和强化供应链合作关系两条途径实现了供应链韧性提升。进一步研究发现：宏观层面，国内生产网络在短期冲击下呈现稀疏化趋势，同时网络内节点的关联紧密性有所增强；微观层面，企业为应对系统性风险冲击倾向于策略性地调整供应链配置，适当提高供应链集中度。在供应链扰动频发的现实背景下，本研究深刻揭示了供应链配置策略选择的情境依赖性，为企业强化供应链治理和提升国内产业链供应链韧性提供了政策启示。

危邦不居：地缘政治冲击与中国企业跨国供应链调整

祝树金、曾聃
地缘政治博弈正在深刻重塑国际经贸秩序与全球生产分工格局，维护产业链供应链安全稳定的重要性日益凸显。本文基于GDELT新闻大数据和FactSet Revere全球供应链数据，构造中国与海外来源国之间的双边地缘政治冲击指标，考察其对中国上市公司跨国供应链调整的影响。研究发现，双边地缘政治冲击显著增加了企业在受影响来源国的供应商关系终止数，使其在该来源国的供应商关系数量净减少；机制检验发现，企业地缘政治风险感知是上述影响的重要传导渠道，形成具体风险预期的企业关系终止数更多；从效率与安全选择偏向来看，地缘政治冲击使企业在终止既有供应商关系时更侧重于安全优先而规避潜在风险。进一步考察企业整体跨国供应链布局发现，企业在短期内主要通过在既有来源国范围内调整供应商组合以应对地缘政治冲击。本研究系统揭示了地缘政治冲击下中国企业跨国供应链调整的特征与路径，为更好统筹发展和安全、防范和应对地缘政治风险提供了政策启示。

Double Strike: How Labor Disruptions Shake and Shape Supply Chains

Dan Zeng and Shujin Zhu
This paper studies the propagation of labor disruptions through supply chains and the role of diversification in shaping resilience. Using data on 5 million supply chain relationships and major work stoppages in the United States, we document three key facts: strikes propagate mainly upstream, causing substantial revenue losses for suppliers while leaving customers largely unaffected; diversification weakens resilience, as the costs of managing diverse networks outweigh risk-sharing benefits; affected suppliers lose more customers and acquire fewer new ones, especially among diversified suppliers. These findings reveal potential vulnerabilities of diversification strategies in risk management.

Supply Chain Configuration Strategies and Resilience Enhancement under Risk ShocksCNKI

Shujin Zhu and Dan Zeng · Economic Research Journal, 2025, 60(8): 94–115

Optimizing supply-chain configuration is an important means of strengthening supply-chain resilience. Taking the global public-health emergency in 2020 as an exogenous shock and using supplier–customer data for Chinese listed firms, this paper examines how supply-chain configuration affects firm resilience under systemic risk. Concentrated configurations significantly mitigate the adverse effects of systemic risk on supply-chain performance. This effect operates through greater supply-chain flexibility and stronger cooperative relationships. At the macro level, domestic production networks become sparser after a short-run shock while ties among remaining nodes become closer. At the micro level, firms respond strategically by moderately increasing supply-chain concentration. The findings highlight the context dependence of supply-chain configuration choices and provide implications for supply-chain governance and resilience.

Avoiding Troubled Countries: Geopolitical Shocks and the Reconfiguration of Chinese Firms’ Global Supply Chains

Shujin Zhu and Dan Zeng
Geopolitical rivalry is profoundly reshaping the international economic and trade order and the global division of production, making the security and stability of industrial and supply chains increasingly important. Using GDELT news data and FactSet Revere global supply-chain data, this paper constructs bilateral geopolitical-shock measures between China and foreign source countries and examines their effects on the cross-border supply-chain adjustments of Chinese listed firms. Bilateral geopolitical shocks significantly increase the number of supplier relationships terminated in the affected source country and reduce firms’ net number of supplier relationships there. Firms’ perceived geopolitical risk is an important transmission channel: firms that form concrete expectations of risk terminate more relationships. When balancing efficiency and security, firms place greater weight on security and avoid potential risks when terminating incumbent supplier relationships. In the short run, firms mainly respond by reconfiguring suppliers within their existing set of source countries. The findings document how Chinese firms adjust cross-border supply chains under geopolitical shocks and provide implications for coordinating development and security and managing geopolitical risk.
